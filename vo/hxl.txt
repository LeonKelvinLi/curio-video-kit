[surprised] A lost hammer led a man to Roman treasure. [excited] Fourteen thousand eight hundred sixty-five coins, and about two hundred pieces of gold and silver, in one Suffolk field. [curious] In nineteen ninety-two, a farmer lost his hammer, and asked a friend with a metal detector to find it. [surprised] The friend, Eric Lawes, filled two carrier bags with treasure, then stopped and reported it. [calm] Archaeologists lifted the rest in a single day, from the remains of a chest buried after the year four oh seven. [excited] It was valued at one point seven five million pounds, and Lawes shared the money with the farmer. [mischievously] And the hammer? The diggers found that too. It went to the British Museum with the treasure.
