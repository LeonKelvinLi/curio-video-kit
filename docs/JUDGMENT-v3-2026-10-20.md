## What the platforms say

- Instagram ranks on **watch time, likes and sends**, with sends counting a little more for reaching non-followers. Mosseri says "the first three seconds of a reel are super important," and skip rate is now the 3-second metric.
- Instagram recommends less of reels that "contain **borders**." Whether a blurred band counts as a border is not stated.
- Safe zone (ads spec, used here because no organic spec exists): keep key content below y≈269, above y≈1248 and 65 px in from each side.
- No length is favored on either platform, so 13–16 s is fine. YouTube judges Shorts on viewed vs swiped away.
- About 75% of Reels are watched with sound on, so the voice carries the hook.
- Instagram's Help Center lists "a reel narrated with a realistic AI-generated voiceover" as content to label "AI info". YouTube doesn't require disclosure for animation with a generic narrator.

## What would optimize them, in order of evidence × effort

1. **Frame 0 = the event, already moving** (all six). Start mid-flip (P1), with the drill punching down at the lamps (P2), with the aisle jam (P4), with the mask dropping or the alarm going off (P5), on the launch (P6). Evidence: your 3-second gate (n=28) and Mosseri.
2. **Open on ~2 s of real, rights-clear material, then cut to the animation** (the Mars pattern). Candidates to verify: NASA public-domain Skylab 3 launch footage (P6); Chilean government CC BY photos of the rescue (P2); a CC photo of deployed oxygen masks (P5); a CC photo of a boarding queue (P4). P1 has no rights-clear footage, so it stays animated.
3. **A visible change at least every ~2 s, especially at 3–6 s.** P5 needs camera moves, warning lights and the passenger reacting. P6's enclosure needs motion. P4's queue needs to start moving at once.
4. **Move P5's timer bar and "this video" marker above y≈1248.**
5. **Swap or reframe P4 and P6** toward a body-at-risk premise. The verified spare is Siffre, the man who lived in a cave without clocks.
6. **Owner decisions:**
   - **(a) Layout.** Your data says the strip caps strong premises. A full-frame cut of P1 and P3 would answer that in one week.
   - **(b) AI label.** Instagram's Help Center says a realistic AI voiceover should be labeled "AI info"; decide whether to add it.
7. **Low priority:** a seamless loop (plausible, unproven) and question endings (untested on your account).
