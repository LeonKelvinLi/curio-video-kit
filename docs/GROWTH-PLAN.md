# How Curio gets more views: research and a ranked plan (2026-10-20)

Three sources, kept separate:
1. **Curio's own numbers**: 12 IG reels, 32 YouTube Shorts, 11 retention curves, 28 reels with Meta skip data.
2. **The niche**: 57 videos pulled from comparable faceless fact/explainer accounts, 40 of them true breakouts (≥20× their channel's median), 3 watched in full.
3. **The platforms**: official Instagram/YouTube/TikTok/Facebook statements first, then large-sample studies.

Raw data is in `evidence/`; the claims and their evidence classes are in `growth-ledger-2026-10-20.json`. Nothing has been posted or changed on any account.

## The short version

Curio's reels are not failing on quality. They fail the **first test round**: Instagram shows every reel to a small sample of non-followers, and only the winners get shown to more people. Curio's reels either pass (about 1.9–2.7K plays) or stop near 200. Everything below is about passing that round more often, and getting more tries at it.
