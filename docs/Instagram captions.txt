CAPTION

In 1966, NASA engineer Jim LeBlanc was testing a spacesuit in a vacuum chamber when a hose came loose. Within seconds he passed out; his last memory was the saliva on his tongue starting to boil. The chamber was refilled in 87 seconds, and he woke up fine. Would you have volunteered for this job? 👇

FIRST COMMENT (sources)
-----------------------
NASA Manned Spacecraft Center, Houston, December 1966. Source: Guinness World Records, "Highest equivalent altitude exposure survived" (pressure equivalent to about 120,000 ft; remembered his saliva boiling; chamber repressurised in 87 seconds; no lasting effects). Curio illustration.
